# Hospital Performance Analysis — Pharmacy & Patient Visits

## Project Overview
This project analyzes hospital performance over one year, focusing on **pharmacy revenue, patient visit distribution across counties, length of stay, drug utilization, and disease diagnosis trends**. The goal is to derive actionable insights that can support operational planning, resource allocation, and healthcare service improvement.

##  Key Insights

### Pharmacy Transactions
- **Total pharmacy transaction cost:** KES 275,000  
- **Total patient visits:** 300  
- Indicates moderate pharmaceutical utilization relative to visit volume.

###  Length of Stay
- **Average length of stay:** 1.4 days  
- Suggests most cases were outpatient or short-term admissions.

###  Patient Visit Distribution by County
- The highest number of patient visits came from **Uasin Gishu County**
- **Total visits from Uasin Gishu:** 88  
- Highlights a major catchment area for the hospital.

###  Drug Utilization
- **Most purchased drugs:** Malaria tablets  
- **Least purchased drugs:** Hypertension medication  
- Reflects disease burden patterns and pharmaceutical demand.

###  Disease Diagnosis Trends
- Peak diagnosis counts occurred in:
  - **March**
  - **August**  
- May indicate seasonal disease patterns.



##  Interpretation
- Malaria remains a dominant health concern within the service population.
- Short average stays suggest efficient treatment or predominantly minor cases.
- County-level analysis reveals geographic concentration of patients.
- Seasonal spikes in diagnoses could inform staffing and inventory planning.



##  Data Scope
The analysis was based on:
- Pharmacy revenue records
- Patient visit logs by county
- Hospital stay durations
- Drug purchase records
- Monthly disease diagnosis counts


##  Potential Applications
- Hospital operational planning
- Pharmaceutical inventory management
- Public health surveillance
- Resource allocation by season
- Regional healthcare strategy development


##  Conclusion
This analysis provides a snapshot of hospital performance over the year, revealing key trends in patient flow, treatment demand, and disease occurrence. These insights can support data-driven decision-making to improve healthcare delivery and efficiency.
